## 🚨 Issues to Watch For

### Critical Issues (Act Immediately)

- ❌ **Server errors (5xx)**: Check Vercel deployment
- ❌ **Security issues**: Review and fix immediately
- ❌ **Manual actions**: Address penalties promptly
- ❌ **Malware detected**: Clean and secure site

### Important Issues (Act Within 24-48 Hours)

- ⚠️ **Crawl errors**: Fix broken links or server issues
- ⚠️ **Coverage drops**: Investigate why pages are being excluded
- ⚠️ **Mobile usability issues**: Fix responsive design problems
- ⚠️ **Structured data errors**: Validate and fix schema markup

### Monitor Issues (Review Weekly)

- 📊 **Indexing delays**: Some pages taking longer than expected
- 📊 **Low impressions**: Limited search visibility
- 📊 **Low CTR**: Meta descriptions may need optimization
- 📊 **High average position**: Rankings lower than desired
