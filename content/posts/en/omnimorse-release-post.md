The launch announcement for OmniMorse, a Morse code translator app for Android and iOS: type, speak or tap a rhythm into Morse, then play it back as sound, flash, screen or vibration, and learn the alphabet.

Built with React Native, Expo and TypeScript on a hexagonal architecture, with Firebase Crashlytics, Storybook, GitHub Actions, CodeQL and Maestro end-to-end tests — 1,363 tests in total. Android's vibration needed its own native Kotlin code. The app is live on the iOS App Store, and the post calls for Android testers to meet the closed-testing requirement.
